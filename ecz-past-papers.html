BEFORE YOU DOWNLOAD

Check the label. Practise with purpose.

Archives can mix different exam routes and years. Confirm the details on the source page before studying from a paper.

  1. GradeMake sure the paper is for Grade 9 or Grade 12.
  2. Year and routeCheck the year and whether it is GCE, internal, external or a specimen.
  3. Subject and paperMatch the subject and paper number to your syllabus or teacher’s guidance.
  4. Marking schemeUse an answer guide only when the archive identifies it for that same paper.
