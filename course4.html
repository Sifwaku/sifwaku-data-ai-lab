ZAMBIA CYBER LAW / DIGITAL PROTECTION

Every lesson follows
the same learning path.

Each module is designed to help students move from legal awareness to real-world action. The learning pattern is consistent: discover the problem, understand the law, study a Zambian scenario, apply a protection method and test mastery through practice.

MODULE 01 Zambia's Cyber-Law Framework

Learn what laws protect people, data and digital systems in Zambia: Cyber Security Act No. 3 of 2025, Cyber Crimes Act No. 4 of 2025, Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021, ZICTA and related institutions.

🎥 Video resources: What is Cyber Law? · Cybersecurity vs Cybercrime · Zambia Cyber Security Act 2025 · Zambia Cyber Crimes Act 2025 · Zambia Data Protection Act 2021

📖 Legal sources: Cyber Security Act 2025 · Cyber Crimes Act 2025 · Data Protection Act 2021 · ZICTA Cyber Security

MODULE 02 Cyber Security Act 2025

Understand cybersecurity governance, incidents, critical information, critical infrastructure, and authorised penetration testing in a Zambian context.

🎥 Video resources: Zambia Cyber Security Act 2025 explained · Zambia Cyber Security Agency · Critical Information Infrastructure explained · Cyber Incident Response explained · Penetration Testing explained

⚠️ Important: security testing must be authorised. Do not test systems you do not own or have explicit permission to test.

📖 Legal source: Cyber Security Act 2025 PDF · Official Parliament page

MODULE 03 Cyber Crimes Act 2025

Explore offence categories such as unauthorised access, interference, cyber-enabled fraud, malicious activity, and child online protection. Distinguish authorised testing from criminal access.

🎥 Video resources: Cybercrime explained · Unauthorised Access / Hacking Explained · Computer Data Interference Explained · Cyber Fraud Explained · Child Online Safety

📖 Primary legal reading: Cyber Crimes Act 2025 — ZambiaLII

MODULE 04 Data Protection & Privacy

Build a personal data map: person → data → purpose → storage → access → sharing → retention → disposal. Learn how to protect names, phone numbers, IDs, financial records and health information.

🎥 Video resources: Personal Data Explained · Data Protection Explained · Data Controller vs Data Processor · Data Subject Rights Explained · Data Breach Explained

📖 Zambian source: Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021

MODULE 05 What Are You Actually Protecting?

Use the SIFWAKU Digital Protection Model: person → data → account → device → application → database → network → organisation. Map asset, threat, vulnerability, risk and control.

🎥 Video resources: How to Protect Personal Information · Password Security · MFA Explained · Device Security · Backup and Disaster Recovery

📖 Support: Data Protection Act 2021 · CISA MFA tips · CISA SMB resources

MODULE 06 Secure Website & Application Development

Audit a small PHP/MySQL or web application. Teach authentication, authorisation, role-based access, password security, validation, SQL injection awareness, session security, encryption, secrets, logging and backups.

🎥 Video resources: OWASP Top 10 2025 — Aikido Security · OWASP Top 10 2025 Announcement · SQL Injection Explained · Web Authentication Security · Secure Coding Practices

📖 Official sources: OWASP Top 10 · OWASP 2021 · OWASP Cheat Sheets

MODULE 07 Social Media, WhatsApp & Digital Identity

Use lived scenarios: fake Facebook accounts, WhatsApp takeover, phishing links, fake scholarship messages, impersonation and malicious attachments. Teach what to do, what is at risk and what evidence to preserve.

🎥 Video resources: Phishing Explained · Social Engineering Explained · WhatsApp Account Security · Identity Theft Explained · Online Scams & Fake Links

📖 Zambian support: ZICTA Cyber Security Awareness

MODULE 08 Children & Online Protection

Cover safeguarding, student data, school systems, harmful content, reporting mechanisms and safer student portal design.

🎥 Video resources: Child Online Safety · Children's Privacy Online · Cyberbullying Explained · Digital Footprint Explained

📖 Legal/support sources: Cyber Crimes Act 2025 · Data Protection Act 2021 · ZICTA awareness

MODULE 09 Cyber Incident Response

Follow the sequence: identify → contain → preserve → document → report → recover → learn. Apply it to a compromised university database or a malicious account takeover.

🎥 Video resources: Cyber Attack Explained · Incident Response for Beginners · Digital Forensics & Evidence · Security Logging & Monitoring

📖 Support sources: Cyber Security Act 2025 · CISA resources · OWASP Top 10

MODULE 10 Digital Protection Audit

Students assess a website, app, school system, NGO system or personal digital environment and deliver a Digital Protection Plan covering assets, risks, access control, backups and incident response.

🎥 Video resources: Cybersecurity Risk Assessment · Vulnerability Assessment Explained · Cybersecurity Audit Explained · NIST Cybersecurity Framework Explained · Web Application Security Testing

📖 Final reference set: Cyber Security Act 2025 · Cyber Crimes Act 2025 · Data Protection Act 2021 · OWASP Top 10 · ZICTA

WHY THIS COURSE EXISTS

Protect people,
data and systems
with clarity and action.

This course teaches the law as it exists in Zambia, while clearly distinguishing legal interpretation from educational guidance. The objective is to help learners understand the rules, identify risks, and respond responsibly before harm happens.

01Learn the legal framework clearly. 02Study practical Zambian scenarios. 03Apply protection methods with evidence.

COURSE NOTE

Law is not only theory.
It is everyday practice.

Students should learn the actual Acts, while examples and security advice are clearly labelled as educational guidance. This keeps the course accurate, practical and suitable for real-life digital decision-making.

COURSE 04 / CORE IDEA PROTECT BEFORE HARM

Build a digital protection plan.

The goal is not to memorise legal terms alone. The goal is to understand risk, protect data, and respond calmly and responsibly when a digital problem occurs.
