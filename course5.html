AI IN BUSINESS / RESPONSIBLE WORK

Use AI to improve work,
not replace judgment.

AI is most useful when it helps a team understand information, generate options, reduce repetitive work or communicate more clearly. It is not a source of automatic truth. This course teaches the working habits that keep speed connected to evidence, accountability and human review.

MODULE 01 What AI Does in a Business

Understand language models, classification, extraction, summarisation, prediction and generation in plain language. Separate an AI capability from a business outcome: faster drafting is not the same as better decisions.

Practice: Rewrite a vague goal such as "use AI in sales" into a measurable outcome such as reducing first-draft proposal time without lowering review quality.

WATCH ON YOUTUBE▶AI in business for beginners▶How generative AI works
MODULE 02 Find High-Value Use Cases

Map repetitive, text-heavy, information-rich tasks where AI can assist: meeting notes, customer-question triage, research briefs, document comparison, idea generation and internal knowledge search.

Avoid: automating a high-impact decision before defining who is accountable, what evidence is required and how a person can challenge the result.

WATCH ON YOUTUBE▶Business AI use cases▶Find automation opportunities
MODULE 03 Work with AI Through Better Instructions

Build a useful request with five parts: role, task, context, constraints and output format. Give examples when the style matters, ask the model to state uncertainty, and provide only the information it needs.

Prompt pattern: "You are helping [role]. Do [task] using [context]. Follow [constraints]. Return [format]. Flag missing evidence and assumptions."

WATCH ON YOUTUBE▶Prompt engineering for business▶How to write better prompts
MODULE 04 Design a Human-AI Workflow

Place AI inside a repeatable process: define the problem, prepare the inputs, generate a draft, inspect the output, test important claims, approve the result and record what happened.

Decide which steps AI may assist, which steps require a trained reviewer, and which decisions must never be delegated without appropriate safeguards.

WATCH ON YOUTUBE▶Human-AI workflow design▶AI product development
MODULE 05 Verify Before You Trust

Learn to check names, numbers, dates, citations, calculations, code, tone and missing perspectives. Compare important outputs with primary sources, small test cases, known examples or a second method.

Rule: fluent language is not evidence. Treat every confident answer as a draft until the claims that matter have been checked.

WATCH ON YOUTUBE▶AI hallucinations and fact-checking▶Evaluate AI output quality
MODULE 06 What to Protect and What to Avoid

Do not paste passwords, private keys, confidential contracts, customer records, identity numbers, health information or unreleased business plans into an AI tool without approved controls.

Watch for fabricated facts, hidden bias, copyright concerns, prompt injection, data leakage, over-automation and decisions that cannot be explained to the people affected.

WATCH ON YOUTUBE▶AI privacy and security risks▶AI bias and responsible use
MODULE 07 Build Team Rules That Work

Create a small AI use policy: approved tools, prohibited data, review levels, disclosure expectations, record keeping, incident reporting and an owner for each workflow.

Teams should know when to say "AI assisted," when to cite a source, when to ask a subject expert, and when to stop using the output.

WATCH ON YOUTUBE▶Responsible AI team policy▶AI governance explained
MODULE 08 Applied Business AI Project

Choose one real workflow and produce an AI opportunity brief. Include the problem, baseline process, proposed workflow, prompt or system design, human checkpoints, risk register, quality test and success measures.

Design a product with AI: research a customer problem, map the user journey, ask AI for several solution concepts, create a low-fidelity prototype, test it with real users and keep the final product decision with the team.

Advertise with AI responsibly: use AI to generate audience hypotheses, campaign angles, copy variants and creative briefs. Check every claim, protect customer data, avoid manipulative targeting and require human approval before publishing.

Use analysis to improve: track reach, click-through rate, conversion rate, cost per acquisition, retention and customer feedback. Compare variants with a clear test, inspect segments carefully and improve the next decision from evidence.

WATCH ON YOUTUBE▶Design products with generative AI▶AI marketing and advertising▶Marketing analytics and conversion

Mastery check: demonstrate that the workflow saves useful time or improves clarity while keeping accuracy, privacy, accountability and human review visible.

WHY THIS COURSE EXISTS

Move from AI curiosity
to responsible capability.

Good AI work is not about pressing a button and accepting whatever comes back. It is about framing a useful problem, giving the system a clear job, checking the result and protecting the people and information involved.

01Find work AI can genuinely improve. 02Verify outputs before they affect people. 03Build safeguards into the workflow.

COURSE NOTE

Speed is useful.
Trust is earned.

The best business use of AI leaves a clear trail: what the task was, what information was used, what the system produced, what a person checked and why the final decision was made.

COURSE 05 / CORE IDEA VERIFY BEFORE DELIVERY

Make the workflow accountable.

AI can draft, sort, compare and suggest. People remain responsible for context, judgment, fairness, privacy and the consequences of action.
