THE SIFFWAKU METHOD / LEARNING LOOP

FROM CURIOSITY TO EVIDENCE

How an idea moves through the lab.

Learning is treated as a process: observe what is happening, investigate it, test what you think, then improve your understanding.

01 / OBSERVE

Notice

Separate what you can see from what you assume.

↘
02 / FRAME

Define the problem

Turn a vague concern into a question that can actually be investigated.

↘
03 / QUESTION

Ask better questions

Break the problem into smaller questions and identify what information is missing.

↘
04 / EVIDENCE

Collect and test

Use data, sources, experiments and reasoning instead of confidence alone.

↘
05 / REVISE

When new evidence changes the picture, change the conclusion too.
