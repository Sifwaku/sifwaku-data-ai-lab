THE LEARNING LOOP

Don't just consume information.
Do something with it.

Use a simple cycle: learn the concept, find a free/open reading companion, make or test something, collect evidence, explain the result and improve.

01 / READFind a legal free book or open textbook.↗02 / UNDERSTANDBreak the concept into clear questions.↗03 / BUILDTurn knowledge into a project or experiment.↗04 / MEASURECollect observations, data or results.↗05 / ANALYSECompare results and explain what happened.↗06 / SHAREDocument the work so it becomes evidence.↗
